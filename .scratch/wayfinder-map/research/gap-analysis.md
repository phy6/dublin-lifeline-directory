## Missing Fields Detail

### Contact Information

- **Phone numbers**: No phone numbers provided for any of the 5 Day Support Centres or 5 Free Doctor Clinics
- **Website URLs**: No websites listed for any location
- **Email addresses**: No email contacts provided
- **Social media**: Not mentioned

### Location Data

- **Coordinates (latitude/longitude)**: Not provided for any location
- **Postal codes**: Only "Dublin 1-8" area codes given, not full Eircodes
- **GPS waypoints**: Not available

### Scheduling Details

- **Doctor clinic specific hours**: The flyer mentions "Mon-Fri with appointment and walk-in times" but does not specify exact hours for each GP clinic
- **MHU schedule**: Mobile Health Unit visits "different locations on scheduled days" but specific days, locations, and times are not detailed
- **Walk-in vs appointment distinction**: Whether each clinic accepts walk-ins, appointments, or both is not clearly specified per clinic
- **Holiday closures**: Not mentioned

### Service Details

- **Service capacity/limits**: No information on how many people can be served
- **Booking requirements**: Whether appointments are needed for specific services (e.g., showers, meals) is not stated
- **Service eligibility criteria**: No information on who qualifies for services
- **Age restrictions**: Not mentioned

### Accessibility & Practical Information

- **Accessibility features**: Wheelchair access, accessible toilets, etc. not specified
- **Languages offered**: Not mentioned
- **What to bring**: Lists of required documents or items not included
- **Parking/transport info**: Not provided
- **Cost/funding details**: Whether services are entirely free or have conditions not stated

### Data Completeness Concerns

- **5th Day Support Centre**: Only 4 Day Support Centres (Mendicity, Capuchin, Lighthouse, Merchants Quay) have full descriptions. The Inclusion Health Hub is listed as a doctor clinic but lacks Day Support Centre hours and full service details
- **MHU specifics**: The Mobile Health Unit schedule (which days, which locations) is referenced but not detailed in the flyer data
- **Doctor clinic schedules**: The flyer states GP clinics operate Mon-Fri with "appointment and walk-in times" but exact time slots are not broken down per clinic

## Recommendations

1. Contact www.primarycaresafetynet.ie for complete GP clinic schedules (day/time/walk-in vs appointment)
2. Obtain MHU route schedule and visiting locations
3. Collect phone numbers and websites for all locations
4. Add coordinates for map functionality
5. Confirm 5th Day Support Centre identity and details
6. Gather accessibility and eligibility information
