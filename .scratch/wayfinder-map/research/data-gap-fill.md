**GP Clinics — Appointment-based:**

- **Monday–Friday, 9:30am – 12:00pm** — Appointment clinics (call 01-2336990 to enquire or visit in person)

**GP Clinics — Walk-in:**

- **Monday–Friday, 1:30pm – 4:00pm** — Walk-in clinics

**Nurse-Led Clinics:**

- Monday–Friday (call 01-2336990 for details)
- Interpreting provided on-site

**Notes:** Open to undocumented migrants, international protection applicants, people experiencing homelessness, and the Roma community. Free of charge. Children seen.

## 4. MHU (Mobile Health Unit) Schedule

### 4a. Safetynet Homeless PC Outreach — Evening MHU (Dublin City)

This is the **Mobile Health Unit** — a van with a fully equipped clinic room that travels to areas where rough sleepers frequent. Operates **three nights per week**.

| Day           | Time             | Location                                        |
| ------------- | ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **Tuesday**   | 7:00pm – 10:00pm | **Mendicity Institute**, 9 Island St, Dublin 8  |
| **Wednesday** | 7:00pm – 10:00pm | **Lighthouse Café**, 28 Pearse Street, Dublin 2 |
| **Thursday**  | 7:00pm – 10:00pm | **Mendicity Institute**, 9 Island St, Dublin 8  |

**Phone:** 087 470 4399 (Homeless PC Outreach)  
**Staff:** GP, nurse, project worker, interpreters available  
**Source:** primarycaresafetynet.ie (homepage + /services/homeless-primary-care-team)

---

### 4b. Safetynet Mobile Health and Screening Unit (MHSU) — For Migrants/Refugees

This is a **separate** MHU — a large vehicle equipped with X-ray and consultation facilities. It screens international protection applicants and refugees for TB, blood-borne viruses, and other conditions at accommodation centres **nationwide**.

**Schedule:** Not publicly available online. The unit travels to various locations across Ireland in partnership with HSE Social Inclusion teams.

**Staff:** Nurses, phlebotomists, GP, radiographer, migrant support workers  
**Phone:** 01 233 6990 (general Safetynet number — ask for MHSU)  
**Source:** primarycaresafetynet.ie/services/mobile-health-and-screening-unit, about.hse.ie

## 5. What I COULDN'T Find

1. **Precise GPS coordinates** for any of the five locations. I used postal district centroids, Wikipedia coordinates, LinkedIn data, and Buildings of Ireland Irish Grid references, but did not obtain exact lat/long from Google Maps API or OSI GeoViewer. For a PWA directory, these should be verified via geocoding.

2. **MHSU (Mobile Health and Screening Unit) schedule.** The nationwide schedule for this unit is not published online. It travels to various accommodation centres across Ireland; specific locations and dates would need to be obtained from Safetynet directly (01-2336990) or from HSE Social Inclusion.

3. **Specific GP clinic hours at the Lighthouse Cafe and Mendicity Institute (as standalone GP clinics).** Neither venue operates its own GP clinic. Safetynet provides outreach/drop-in services at these locations, but there is no dedicated GP clinic with independent hours at either address.

4. **Lighthouse Café phone number specificity.** Multiple numbers exist for Dublin Christian Mission and The Lighthouse Café. The +353 89 707 8166 number (from Waze/Wheree) appears to be the direct café line, while +353 1 677 5548 is the Dublin Christian Mission HQ at Chancery Place. These may or may not be the same organisation's contact points for this specific service.

5. **Whether the MQI GP clinic hours listed on the HPCT page and the MQI healthcare page are current/reconciled.** There are discrepancies between the two sources (e.g., HPCT says Mon 9am-12pm and Mon 2pm-4:30pm; MQI says Mon 9:30am-12pm and 1:30pm-3:30pm). These may reflect different services at the same building or different time periods. The MQI website appears more recently updated.

6. **Sunday/evenings for most GP services.** None of the Safetynet GP clinics operate on Sundays. Evening services are limited to the MHU outreach (Tues/Wed/Thu 7pm–10pm). No weekend GP clinics were found at any of the five locations.

7. **Mendicity Institute's own website/menu for GP services.** Mendicity (mendicity.org) does not appear to list GP clinic information on their public-facing site. The only GP service listed is the Safetynet HPCT drop-in on Wednesday mornings.
